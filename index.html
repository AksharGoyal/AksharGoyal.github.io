About

I'm a data engineer at Adeptmind. Most of my work is building and maintaining scrapers that collect product and stock data from retailer websites, in Python, TypeScript and Go.

Lately that has meant fixing stale stock data, cutting down noisy alerts, making scrapers more reliable, and clearing security issues ahead of our SOC 2 audit.

I studied statistics at the University of Waterloo and have built ML and LLM projects on the side. I use AI tools when I code, and I review every change before I push it.
